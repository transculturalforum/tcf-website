Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use this website, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Transcultural Forum Inc. ("we", "us") is a registered non-profit organization. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at Transculturalforum@gmail.com.

Information you give us

We only collect personal information that you choose to send us through the forms on this website:

  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, phone number (optional) and message.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.
  • Volunteer form: your name, email address, phone number, areas of interest and message.
  • Share your culture story: your name, email address, the culture you are writing about and your story.
  • Free Dialogue submissions: your name, email address, the type of submission, topic, sources and message.

These forms are processed by Formspree, a service that delivers form submissions to our email inbox.

Donations

Online donations are handled by Zeffy on its own secure page. Zeffy collects your name, contact details and payment information so it can process your gift and send your tax receipt. We never see or store your full card details. Zeffy shares your name, email and gift amount with us so we can thank you and keep the records a non-profit must keep.

If you give by Zelle, your bank shares your name and the payment details with us. We use them only to record your gift and send your receipt.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To send our newsletter, if you signed up for it.
  • To organize volunteers and events.
  • To review stories and dialogue submissions. If we publish a submission, we will only use your name with it if you agree.
  • To process, record and acknowledge donations, and to meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share it with the services listed on this page, which help us run the website, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. When you visit, your browser loads some content from other services: icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and photos from Pexels. Like any website, these services may record your IP address and browser type to deliver that content. Zeffy and Formspree may use cookies on their own pages to keep payments and forms secure.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and volunteer details only as long as we need them to respond or work with you. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Donation records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you emails. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe option, or you can email Transculturalforum@gmail.com and we will act on your request promptly.

Children

This website is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
